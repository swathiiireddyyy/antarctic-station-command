# Antarctic Station Command — 16 Modules Overview

The platform provides comprehensive monitoring and command operations for India's Antarctic stations: **Maitri** (Schirmacher Oasis) and **Bharati** (Larsemann Hills).

| # | Module | Key Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dashboard** | Real-time overview of indoor/outdoor temperature, power generation mix, fuel/water levels, wind speed, live KPI cards, and active warning alerts. |
| 2 | **HQ Overview** | Multi-station situational map showing geographical positions, live weather summaries, crew complements, and inter-station comms latency. |
| 3 | **Energy** | Generation mix (solar, wind, diesel generators, battery storage), live renewable share computation `(Solar + Wind) / Total`, net surplus/deficit indicators, and hourly load graphs. |
| 4 | **Inventory** | Critical consumables tracker (fuel, potable water, rations, oxygen, medical supplies), burn-rate calculations, and resupply countdown. |
| 5 | **Equipment** | Machinery health indices, run-time hours, real-time maintenance due dates, service scheduling, and predictive breakdown alerts. |
| 6 | **Environment** | Outside meteorology, barometric pressure trends, UV index, wind chill factor, solar irradiance, and ozone column monitoring. |
| 7 | **Personnel & Safety** | Crew manifest, isolated three-tab navigation (Personnel Roster, Crew Wellness Tracker, Medical Protocol & Treaty compliance). |
| 8 | **Digital Twin** | 2.5D station schematic showing building modules color-coded by subsystem (power, hab, science, life support, comms) with live sensor overlays. |
| 9 | **Connectivity** | Satellite link telemetry, uplink/downlink throughput, SNR, packet loss, store-and-forward queue monitor, and Link Outage simulator. |
| 10 | **Alerts** | Priority matrix (Critical / Warning / Advisory), real-time alarm muting/unmuting, acknowledge, and resolve workflow. |
| 11 | **Emergency** | SOS protocol trigger, evacuation route planner, shelter-in-place mode, life-support life-raft counters, and distress beacon activation. |
| 12 | **Simulations** | Interactive blizzard simulator, generator trip test, satellite blackout drill, and emergency power rationing scenarios. |
| 13 | **Logbook** | Official station log entries, shift handovers, scientific expedition logs, tamper-evident audit timestamps, and exportable records. |
| 14 | **Analytics** | Historical trend charts, solar vs wind yield correlations, heating degree days (HDD), and efficiency benchmarks. |
| 15 | **Reports** | Automated compliance reporting for the Antarctic Treaty System (ATS), Madrid Protocol environmental impact, and NCPOR executive briefings. |
| 16 | **Settings** | Sound alert synthesis toggle, auto-refresh cadence, offline banner preference, dark/polar mode styling, and data retention policies. |
