01  Project Overview

Introduction
"India's Antarctic stations operate in one of the world's most extreme environments — where real-time situational awareness, predictive intelligence, and rapid emergency response are not optional, but critical for safety and mission success."
Problem Statement
  • Fragmented monitoring — sensors, equipment, and personnel tracked in silos
  • Reactive maintenance — equipment failures discovered after the fact
  • Limited situational awareness — no unified real-time picture of station health
  • Emergency response gaps — no integrated alert-to-action pipeline
  • No predictive capability — supply and equipment shortfalls not anticipated
  • Harsh connectivity — systems must work offline in polar conditions
Our Solution
  • Unified Digital Twin — single dashboard for all station systems
  • AI Predictive Engine — failure risk scoring & supply forecasting
  • 3-Level Alert System — automated threshold monitoring with notifications
  • Emergency Command — SOS, evacuation maps, protocols integrated
  • What-If Simulations — test scenarios before they happen
  • Offline-first design — full functionality without internet

Target Stations
🏔️ Maitri Station — Queen Maud Land

India's second Antarctic station, established 1989. Year-round operations at 70°46'S, 11°44'E. Team of 25 personnel in summer, 8–12 in winter. Primary focus: atmospheric science, geology, biology.

🏔️ Bharati Station — Larsemann Hills

India's third Antarctic station, commissioned 2012. Located at 69°24'S, 76°11'E. Modern containerized design, energy-efficient. Focus: marine science, remote sensing, glaciology.

🤖 How AI Works

The platform uses rule-based heuristic intelligence with time-series drift simulation:

  • Sensor Drift Engine — each sensor follows realistic ±drift patterns over time
  • Threshold Monitoring — multi-level alert triggers with 5-minute cooldown
  • Consumption Rate — daily consumption rates used to forecast critical dates
  • Failure Risk Score — health % × hours × random drift → 0–1 risk index
  • Anomaly Baseline — sensor values outside ±15% of normal range flagged

Scalability & Extensibility
🌊 Other NCPOR Missions

Architecture designed to extend to Arctic expeditions (Himadri), ocean research vessels (ORV Sagar Nidhi), and coastal monitoring stations.

🤝 API-Ready Backend

REST API architecture allows plug-in of real IoT sensor streams, external weather APIs (ECMWF), satellite imagery, and third-party alert channels (SMS, email).

🔬 Research Data

All sensor time-series data can be exported for scientific analysis, shared with international polar research bodies (SCAR, COMNAP), and published as open datasets.
