What is This Platform?

The Antarctic Research Station Digital Twin is a real-time, AI-powered monitoring and operations platform built for India's polar research stations. It creates a complete digital mirror of the physical station — all sensors, equipment, personnel, environment, energy systems, and emergency protocols — accessible from a single unified dashboard, anywhere, anytime.

India operates two research stations in Antarctica — Maitri (since 1989) and Bharati (since 2012). Personnel stationed there face extreme conditions: temperatures as low as −50°C, wind speeds exceeding 150 km/h, months of polar night, and complete isolation from the outside world. Managing infrastructure, energy, safety, and research in such conditions requires intelligent, always-on digital tools.
This platform digitally replicates the station and provides operators with live sensor data, predictive maintenance alerts, AI-driven recommendations, emergency response workflows, and supply chain forecasting — all in one place, working even without internet connectivity.

Core Features
  • Real-Time Dashboard — Live KPIs: power generation, fuel, battery, temperature, personnel status, active alerts, equipment health
  • Energy Management — Track solar, wind, diesel generation; optimize load distribution; battery forecasting
  • Equipment & Maintenance — Health scoring (0–100%) for all 10 critical equipment; work orders; AI failure risk prediction
  • Inventory Forecasting — Stock levels, expiry tracking, AI-powered 30-day consumption forecasts for 10 item categories
  • Environmental Monitoring — Temperature, wind, humidity, pressure, CO₂, smoke, CO, AQI with anomaly detection
  • Personnel Safety — Real-time inside/outside tracking, safety status, weather-aware risk alerts
  • 3-Level Alert System — Critical (red), Warning (yellow), Info (blue) with auto-triggers, acknowledge, and resolve workflow
  • Emergency Response — SOS button, one-click fire/blizzard/medical alerts, evacuation map, step-by-step protocols
  • Interactive Digital Twin — Clickable 2D station map; click any building to see live sensor data and personnel count
  • What-If Simulations — 8 pre-built disaster scenarios: generator failure, blizzard, fuel shortage, comms outage, fire, water failure
  • Reports & Logbook — Auto-scheduled daily/weekly/monthly reports; digital station diary with category-based search
  • Analytics KPIs — Energy, inventory, equipment, and safety analytics with trend charts and 7-day history
